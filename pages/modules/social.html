Social: Authentic Milestone Celebration

An anti-dopamine community feed engineered strictly for verified accomplishments. Broadcast sprint finishes, habit streak breakthroughs, and constructive peer encouragement without ads or endless algorithmic rabbit holes.

The Digital Breakdown

Addictive Doom-Scrolling & Envy Feeds

Mainstream platforms (Instagram, TikTok) engineer outrage, envy, and infinite scrolling to hijack attention. Users open their phones to connect but end up losing hours of creative energy.

The NextGen Solution

Action-Backed Proof & Finite Feeds

Social only allows posts backed by verified action: completed sprints, 30-day streak milestones, or study hours logged. No clickbait, no sponsor ads, and no addictive engagement baiting.
