MODULE #09 • ACCOUNTABILITY NETWORK COACHING HUB

Mentorship: Guided Accountability

Bridge the discipline gap with formal mentor-mentee partnerships. Share selective roadmap visibility, review weekly velocities, and receive verified encouragement nudges.

The Digital Breakdown

Unstructured Advice & Fragmented Coaching

Mentorship typically collapses into random texting without context. Mentors cannot see what their mentees are actually doing every day, leaving guidance superficial and ineffective.

The NextGen Solution

Live Objective Telemetry & Access Permissions

Mentorship provides structured visibility into specific task buckets or sprint roadmaps. Mentors review real completion curves, leave milestone reviews, and send targeted encouragement prompts.

Operational Flow

How Mentorship Works

1

Handshake Pairing

Connect via secure QR code or invitation link with configurable granular privacy permissions.

2

Choose which modules to share (e.g. Study sprint tasks shared, while personal diary remains locked).

3

Milestone Review

Mentors verify milestone completion, leave audio/text feedback, and approve phase advancements.

4

Encouragement Alerts

Receive real-time motivational nudges when you enter focus sessions or successfully conclude sprints.
