Dr. Fogg demonstrates that big changes occur by creating tiny habits anchored to existing routines. When you tie a new action to an established neural anchor, friction approaches zero.

The Habit Stacking Formula

Coined by James Clear and grounded in Dr. B.J. Fogg's Stanford research, habit stacking uses an established habit as the automatic trigger for a new behavior:

📌
The Universal Formula:

“After I [CURRENT HABIT], I will [NEW ATOMIC HABIT].”

Example: “After I finish my morning coffee, I will open Time Chart and review my Day Tasks for 2 minutes.”

This works because existing habits have deeply ingrained synaptic connections. Anchoring onto them borrows that momentum without exhausting finite conscious willpower.

The B=MAP Behavior Model

Dr. Fogg proved that human behavior ($B$) occurs only when three factors converge simultaneously:

FACTOR 1

Motivation ($M$)

Your desire to act. High motivation fluctuates wildly; Time Chart expects motivation to be low and designs accordingly.

FACTOR 2

Ability ($A$)

How easy the task is. Time Chart breaks tasks into micro-subtasks so the ability threshold is effortless.

FACTOR 3

Prompt ($P$)

The anchor trigger. Smart Notifications deliver prompts at the exact window of opportunity.
