What Is Flow State?

Psychologist Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi defined flow as the state of complete absorption in an intrinsically rewarding, challenging task. Key characteristics include:

  • Dissolution of ego and internal self-doubt
  • Distorted time perception (four hours vanish like twenty minutes)
  • Intrinsic enjoyment: the activity is its own reward
  • Laser focus unbothered by ambient environmental noise
  • Effortless flow between action and awareness
