What Is Kaizen?

Kaizen (改善) is a Japanese word meaning “change for better.” In modern organizational psychology, it refers to the philosophy of continuous, incremental improvement — small daily enhancements across all processes rather than infrequent radical overhauls.

Applied to personal productivity, Kaizen translates to:

  • Improving your workflow today, even by just 1%
  • Reviewing and iterating on your schedule constantly
  • Never accepting friction or disorder as unavoidable
  • Making process mastery the goal, not just stressful deadlines
🏭
The Toyota Production Proof

Toyota's application of Kaizen transformed it from a struggling post-WWII manufacturer into the world's premier automaker. The same principle — applied daily to personal routines — produces unstoppable compounding over months and years.

Know Your Customer (Future You)
