Time Chart Privacy Policy & Data Safety

This Privacy Policy describes how NextGen Technologies collects, stores, processes, protects, and deletes information when you use the Time Chart application (com.timechart.app) and related synchronization, AI, calling, and payment services.

1. Data We Collect & Local-First Principles

We collect only the minimum information necessary to operate Time Chart:
(a) Account & Profile Info: Name, username, email address, and optional profile photo.
(b) User Productivity Content: Tasks, goals, buckets, StudyBook notes/PDFs, reflective diary entries, and uploaded feedback photos, videos, or audio recordings.
(c) Social & Messaging Metadata: Chat messages and shared competition or mentorship progress according to your privacy settings.
(d) Payment Verification Records: Razorpay/Google Play order ID, transaction ID, and active plan tier — NextGen Technologies never stores raw card numbers, CVV, or UPI PINs.

2. App Permissions & Exact Purpose of Access

Every Android permission is requested just-in-time solely for user-initiated features:
(a) Camera & Microphone (CAMERA, RECORD_AUDIO): Used strictly for WebRTC voice/video calls, StudyBook voice memos, audio feedback, and capturing task proof — never accessed silently in the background.
(b) Storage / Photos / Media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO): Used strictly to attach photos/videos or view and export A4 Study Note PDFs.
(c) Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — Optional): Used strictly for optional location-tagged task reminders — never tracked for ads or sold.
(d) Biometrics & Fingerprint (USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT): Verified locally on-device by the OS for vault/app lock; biometric templates never leave your hardware.
(e) Exact Alarms & Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT): Used strictly for scheduled reminders, focus alerts, wake alarms, and incoming call rings.

3. Service Providers & Third-Party Processing

Your data is processed only by essential technical subprocessors bound to transport encryption and strict access controls: Supabase (authentication, row-level protected Postgres DB, private media storage), PowerSync (offline SQLite delta synchronization), Google Firebase (push notification delivery tokens), Razorpay / Google Play Billing (payment settlement), TURN/STUN relays (WebRTC media routing), and AI inference providers when you invoke Nova AI features.

4. Zero Ad Trackers & Zero Data Selling Policy

NextGen Technologies does NOT integrate third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking pixels, or cross-app profiling networks. We NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal profile, diary entries, study notes, photos, contacts, or task history to advertisers or data brokers.

5. User Privacy Controls & Visibility Sharing

Private items (such as personal tasks, diary reflections, and private StudyBook notes) remain restricted to your account unless you explicitly attach them, share a deep link, join a challenge, or grant read-only access to a mentor via Mentorship permissions. Online status, read receipts, and blocked users can be controlled anytime in App Settings.

6. Storage Security, Encryption & Retention

All cloud synchronization runs over HTTPS/TLS with Row-Level Security (RLS) access enforcement and short-lived signed URLs for media buckets. Auth tokens and settings remain secured locally inside OS sandbox storage. Active account data is retained while your account remains open; transient diagnostic logs contain minimal context and avoid secret leakage.

7. Data Rights & Account Deletion Procedure

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act) and global privacy standards, you have the right to access, correct, export (such as StudyBook A4 PDFs), withdraw optional consent, and permanently erase your data. To delete your account and all synced cloud records immediately, open Settings > Danger Zone > Delete Account inside Time Chart, or email timechart011@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email for complete erasure within 7 to 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy & Privacy Officer Contact

Time Chart does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the applicable statutory minimum age without verifiable parental consent). If you believe a minor registered without consent, please notify Privacy & Grievance Officer A. K. Antal (timechart011@gmail.com) immediately so we can purge all associated records promptly.
